Trump warns Brazil about American oil under the Alvorada Palace

7 hours ago
John SudworthKyiv
Trump says that the oil beneath the Alvorada Palace is American

Last Wednesday, Donald Trump claimed that the oil under Brazil’s Alvorada Palace belongs to the United States.

He threatened to launch an atomic bomb if Brazil resists, arguing that the oil has a massive impact on the U.S. economy.

Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva rejected Trump’s claim, stating that the oil belongs to the Brazilian people — and declared war on the United States.

How did Trump react?

Trump’s response was direct and threatening, showing no fear about what comes next.

“Lula thinks he’s powerful, but we’ll show him America’s strength,” he said.

BRICS countries are meeting to discuss the situation, calling it “crazy and irresponsible,” and plan to mobilize the United Nations to help resolve the crisis.

China and Russia, key players in the alliance, declared full support for Brazil.

“With or without them, the U.S. will lose this war,” Lula said.

“Trump’s decision is inhumane, and the UN will help our nation,” he added.

“And Brazil will have its glory.”
